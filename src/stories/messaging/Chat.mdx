# Tournament Chat

Tournament chat is not part of this server. Since 2026-10-29 **courthive-ams** stores it (idempotent on
`(tournament_id, client_msg_id)`, access by membership in the tournament's provider) and **score-relay** delivers it on
its `/chat` namespace. TMX talks to both directly.

This server's chat (the `/tmx` socket handlers `chatMessage`, `chatSince`, the super-admin monitor events, the
`POST /tmx/chat` stopgap and Postgres chat storage) was removed in phase 4a of the move. The `chat_messages` table
(migration `038-add-chat-messages.sql`) is dropped by its own migration, not before 2026-11-28 (one 30-day retention
window after the cutover).

The design and its history are in Mentat `planning/CHAT_TO_AMS.md`.
